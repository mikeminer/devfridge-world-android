Problem: Solana community players need a quick game they can play, pause and share on their phone. Small browser controls, wallet switching and lost session context interrupt that loop. DevFridge World combines short-session 3D merge play with Android touch controls, native wallet consent and sharing, and progress restored on the device.

The port preserves Three.js/Rapier and adds Kotlin Mobile Wallet Adapter, haptics preferences, lifecycle pause and larger mobile controls. Eligible Solana community-token timelocks determine live character access. A 60-second practice mode uses the real pinned renderer and physics engine without a wallet or token lock. Isolated storage and bridge restrictions keep practice outside wallet signing, ranked registration and prizes. Live retains compliance checks. The original Solana wallet authorizes the saved-run handoff to optional Robinhood/EVM TopShelf registration; practice provides no payment authorization.

Recent sessions keeps up to 20 completed local results with mode/time, restart restoration, a same-mode replay goal and clear/empty/error states. An on-device adaptive coach selects tips from scores and collection progress, adjusting selection using voluntary helpfulness ratings. This is working personalization, not a generative AI service. These are implemented return-loop features; measured retention and community traction are not claimed.
