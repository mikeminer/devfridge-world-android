Problem: Solana community players need a quick game they can play, pause and share on their phone. Small browser controls, wallet switching and lost session context interrupt that loop. DevFridge World brings short-session 3D merge play, Android touch controls, native consent/sharing and restored progress to that mobile experience.

The Android app preserves Three.js/Rapier and adds Kotlin Mobile Wallet Adapter, haptics preferences, lifecycle pause and local history. Beta.2/code9 includes 60-second local practice with the real pinned renderer/physics engine. Practice needs no wallet or token lock; isolated storage and bridge restrictions prevent wallet/blockchain requests, ranked registration and prizes. Live retains compliance and eligible community-token timelock access. Original-wallet approval supports the Solana side of the separate Robinhood/EVM TopShelf handoff; practice grants no eligibility or payment authorization.

Recent sessions stores at most 20 completed local results with timestamps and mode, restores after restart, suggests a same-mode replay goal and provides empty/error states and clear history. An on-device adaptive coach selects tips from score/collection progress and voluntary helpfulness ratings. This is a personalization algorithm, not a generative AI service. Retention and community traction have not been measured.
