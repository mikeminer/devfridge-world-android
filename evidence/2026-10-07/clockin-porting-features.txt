Problem: Solana community players need short game sessions they can play, pause and share on a phone. Small browser controls, wallet handoffs and lost session context interrupt that loop. DevFridge World preserves Three.js/Rapier 3D merge play and adds Kotlin MWA authorization/exact-message signing, larger touch targets, haptics preferences, native sharing and lifecycle recovery.

The live loop connects a Solana community identity to the playable cast: the selected character's mint must have at least 500,000 actively timelocked tokens, summed in exact raw units for the authorized wallet. The existing backend checks access and verifies completed runs; optional seasonal TopShelf registration links the original Solana authorization to a separate Robinhood/EVM account. The ten character cards also embed live DevFridge Scan badges: each mint's active timelocked supply percentage links to its scanner. All ten SVGs loaded successfully; they measure token commitment, not game sessions. A labelled 60-second practice round teaches the pinned engine without a wallet or lock. Isolated storage and bridge restrictions keep practice outside signing, ranked registration and prizes.
