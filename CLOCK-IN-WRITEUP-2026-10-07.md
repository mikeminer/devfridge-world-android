## Evidence interpretation

Fridge's existing shared Solana program supplies the economic commitment behind the live community loop. The game-builder skill supplies per-game accepted-mint, raw-threshold and duration configuration. There is no on-chain GameID registry; a wallet can qualify for two games accepting its mint and rules, so their counts are not additive as ecosystem-unique wallets. The live app uses wallet-selected character access, visible per-mint badges, server verification and optional Robinhood seasonal score registration. Local practice supplies separate onboarding.

[On-chain metrics, definitions and receipts](ONCHAIN-METRICS-2026-10-07.md) distinguish game wallets with timelocked access, per-mint locked amount / total supply, the 13/19 = 68.42% active-lock account fraction, post-expiry token-commitment renewal and optional registered-score participants. Different mints' raw amounts never combine; the eligible-wallet union deduplicates wallets across characters. Current-policy reconstruction finds one observed treasury renewing wallet in both the 7- and 30-day windows, with zero observed external renewing wallets; this applies today's rule retrospectively rather than proving historical game eligibility. Missing claimed/closed accounts prevent a complete historical renewal rate. The [offline calculator](evidence/2026-10-07/measurement/README.md) adds no live collector or existing game/backend/APK change.

Repeated TopShelf owner registrations are labelled publisher activity. Zero external wallets in the access snapshot or registered-score interval does not establish zero overall game users. These token-commitment measurements are independent of play frequency and contain no Android platform attribution.
